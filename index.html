Welcome
Who we are

Why BSAUU exists

The Bangladesh Student Association at the University of Utah is the connecting hub for Bangladeshi students on campus. We share our culture with the wider university, look after students arriving in Utah for the first time, and make sure nobody has to spend Eid or Pohela Boishakh alone.

Culture we can touch

Boishakh, Eid, Victory Day and Ekushey — celebrated properly, with the food, music and clothes that go with them.

Music, sport and evenings out

Cricket on weekends, watch parties, potlucks and open-mic nights — the ordinary things that turn a campus into a community.

A hand for new students

Airport pickups, first-week questions, housing advice and the people who have already been through it.
