Welcome to BSAUU

The mission of the Bangladeshi Student Association at the University of Utah is to serve as a connecting hub for Bangladeshi students, promote and share Bangladeshi culture with the University of Utah community, and create a welcoming environment for new students.

Community

Building a strong, supportive network for students to feel at home on campus and exchange ideas.

Culture

Celebrating our rich heritage through festivals, food, music, and traditional events.

Support

Assisting new and prospective students with information and resources for a smooth transition to Utah.
